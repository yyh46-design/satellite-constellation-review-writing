## English highlights

- Distinguishes service-completion criteria across constellation applications.
- Compares joint, sequential, and feedback decisions by revision authority.
- Relates scalability to preparation, coordination, and activation costs.
- Connects model outputs to documented events and validation environments.

## 贡献说明

The review compares constellation management methods by the service outcome being rewarded, the decisions that remain revisable, and the information and execution conditions required for revisions. Representative observation, communication, maintenance, and recovery studies reveal how similar method labels can conceal different completion events, statistical populations, and costs. The resulting synthesis identifies conditional comparisons that can be tested on common task and disturbance histories, while distinguishing model, software, measurement, and flight evidence.
